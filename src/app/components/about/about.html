ABOUT & CREDITS

Abandoned, but not forgotten.

Since the 1960s, NASA has left machines on the Moon, on Mars and far beyond the planets. Some went quiet decades ago. A few are still talking. Ask the Machines lets young explorers meet {{ machineCount }} of them, hear their stories and find out what each one taught us.

We built it for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge, for the challenge Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars.

Real data from NASA

Every story is built on NASA records. The {{ machineCount }} stories carry {{ factCount }} checked facts, and each fact links to the page it came from. Open any story and tap Fact check to see them.
